Playlist Subtitles

Downloads English subtitles of a whole YouTube playlist as one .txt file. It runs on the YouTube page itself, so nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Option A: Bookmarklet (easiest)

  1. Drag this button to your bookmarks bar: Get playlist subtitles
  2. Open your playlist on YouTube.
  3. Click the bookmark. A small box shows progress, then a .txt file downloads.

Bookmarks bar hidden? Press Ctrl+Shift+B (Cmd+Shift+B on Mac).

Option B: Console (if the bookmarklet is blocked)

  2. Open your playlist on YouTube and press F12, then open the Console tab.
  3. Firefox/Chrome may ask you to type allow pasting first. Then paste the script and press Enter.
The playlist page must be open in the same tab you run it in. Videos without an English transcript are skipped and listed in the output file.
